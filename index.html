About INCHARA MASTER SOUNDS

Founded and owned by Gajanan Ishwar Patagar, INCHARA MASTER SOUNDS is a registered Micro, Small and Medium Enterprise (MSME) headquartered in Sirsi, Uttara Kannada, Karnataka. We specialize in professional live sound reinforcement, high-power speaker stack deployment, audio crossover calibration, event recreation services (NIC 93290), and companion Android software utilities designed for event, audio, and productivity workflows.

Our Core Offerings

Live Sound Reinforcement

Full-scale PA setups, high-output subwoofers, active 2-way/3-way crossover management, power amplifier racks, and wireless microphone systems for concerts and events.

Audio & System Engineering

Custom loudspeaker enclosure tuning, multi-channel mixing console operation, acoustic staging, and electrical power distribution for outdoor and indoor venues.

Mobile Apps & Digital Tools

Development and publishing of official Android applications and utility software on Google Play under the INCHARA MASTER SOUNDS developer identity.
